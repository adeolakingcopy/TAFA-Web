02 / Identity

The Meaning of Demure

The word "demure" is associated with understated elegance, composure, and restraint. For DEMURE MODELS, it represents dignity, intentional self-presentation, and absolute professionalism — not a limitation on individuality, creativity, or personal expression.

This philosophy applies across every category, age group, and body type. The agency's emphasis is on carrying oneself with confidence, respecting others on demanding sets, and delivering value to client campaigns without arrogance.

05 / Ethics

Model Welfare & Safeguards

DEMURE models adheres to documented professional standards grounded in dignity, accountability, and the protection of our talent.

Child Safeguarding

Applications, training, and bookings involving minors require verified parent or legal guardian involvement, documented safeguards, and strict working hour limits.

Transparent Written Terms

Written agreements explain representation scope, commissions, payment arrangements, and image usage rights upfront. No hidden fees or guaranteed-earnings fallacies.

Model Safety & Boundaries

Assignment details—wardrobe, concepts, shoot location, and duration—are disclosed in advance. Models retain the right to decline non-agreed changes without intimidation.

Image Rights & AI Protections

Photographs and footage are used solely within agreed commercial parameters with explicit permissions and protections against unauthorized synthetic reproduction.
