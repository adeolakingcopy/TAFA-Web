Yes. The 2-Day Virtual AI Mastery Class is fully subsidized by TalentFlow Africa as part of our mission to build digital and AI capability across the continent. There are no hidden fees or credit card requirements.

No. Day 1 starts with foundational, intuitive frameworks that any professional can understand. Day 2 introduces no-code automation and workflow tools that do not require software engineering knowledge.

We work with ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Zapier, Make, and Google Workspace AI integrations. You only need a free account on these platforms to participate in the exercises.

Yes! Attendees who participate in both days and complete the Day 2 mini capstone challenge will receive an official, verifiable TAFA Academy Certificate of Attendance.
